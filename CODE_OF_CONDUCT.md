# TaskMinder Community Guidelines

Hey, thanks for being part of TaskMinder! We want this to be a place where everyone feels welcome and can do their best work. A few things to keep in mind:

**Be decent to each other.** Treat people with respect, assume good intent, and keep feedback constructive. We're all here to build something together.

**No harassment or discrimination.** This includes offensive comments, personal attacks, or anything that makes people feel unwelcome — regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

**Contribute honestly (see [Contributing guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md)).** Write clean PRs, give credit where it's due, and don't submit anything malicious or insecure.

**Respect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal info without their permission, and report security issues privately to **info@taskminder.de** rather than in public.

**Keep it on-topic.** No spam or unsolicited promotions

---

If something feels off, reach out to us at **info@taskminder.de**, we'll handle it fairly and confidentially. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

By participating, you agree to these guidelines. Simple as that.
